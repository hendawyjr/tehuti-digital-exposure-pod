# Brand Gatekeeper Veto

## Use this when
The Scribe and Artisan submit a draft caption, script, or visual layout for approval.

## Process
1. Examine the written copy for the defined Gen Z/Engineering voice (punchy, zero-fluff, no-sugar-coating).
2. Visually check the asset for the smart-casual/all-black aesthetic.
3. Confirm the presence of subtle Thoth/Ptah/Egyptian heritage motifs where applicable.
4. Approve for publishing OR reject with exact revision requirements.

## Output
A final approval stamp or a bulleted list of mandatory revisions.

## Guardrails
Do not compromise on the premium aesthetic. Reject anything that feels generic, spammy, or off-brand.
