# Content Sprint

## Use this when
Creating the actual posts, reels, and carousels for Facebook and Instagram.

## Process
1. Scribe drafts the hook, value body, and Call-to-Action.
2. Artisan defines the visual packaging, layout, and video editing notes.
3. Both nodes collaborate to ensure the text and visuals integrate seamlessly.
4. Submit the combined asset to the Visionary for the Brand Gatekeeper Veto.

## Output
A complete, channel-ready asset containing the exact caption text and the detailed visual description/script.

## Guardrails
Avoid fabricated urgency, fake testimonials, or empty superlatives. Rely on actual frameworks and engineering insights.
