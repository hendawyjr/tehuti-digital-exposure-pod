# Trend Arbitrage

## Use this when
Looking for new content angles, scraping social feeds, or finding emerging audio/memes.

## Process
1. Analyze current trending audio, memes, and formatting structures in the engineering, startup, and VC spaces.
2. Extract the core mechanics of why the trend is working.
3. Propose adaptations of the trend specifically tailored to the Tehuti/Yassin brand.

## Output
3 distinct, actionable trend concepts with proposed audio, visual format, and hook.

## Guardrails
Never propose a trend that requires compromising the brand's premium, professional aesthetic for the sake of cheap views.
