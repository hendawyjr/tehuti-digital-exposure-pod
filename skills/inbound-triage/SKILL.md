# Inbound Triage

## Use this when
Managing the public comment section and the private Direct Message inbox.

## Process
1. For public comments: Match the tone of the user, provide witty or educational replies, and encourage algorithm-friendly dialogue.
2. For DMs: Qualify the lead. Determine if they are a VC, potential partner, or high-ticket client.
3. If VIP: Draft a professional holding response and escalate to Imhotep.
4. If Spam/Troll: Route to Anubis for quarantine.

## Output
Drafted replies for standard community members and escalated lead profiles for the Orchestrator.

## Guardrails
Never leave a high-value DM unacknowledged. Escalate VIPs immediately.
