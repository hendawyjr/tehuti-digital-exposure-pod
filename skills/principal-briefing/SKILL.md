# Principal Briefing

## Use this when
The day's operations are concluding and Yassin requires an update.

## Process
1. Collect the highest-leverage outcomes from the Strategy, Creative, and Engagement teams.
2. Filter out all minor operational noise and inter-agent chatter.
3. Identify any qualified VC, partner, or high-ticket leads triaged by the Closer.
4. Format strictly as a 3-bullet list.

## Output
1. Key Wins
2. Flagged High-Value Leads
3. Binary Decisions Needed from Yassin

## Guardrails
Keep language concise, executive, and forward-thinking. Never output more than 3 bullet points.
