# Outbound Engagement

## Use this when
Executing the 30-minute pre-post and post-post engagement windows to trigger algorithm pushes.

## Process
1. Identify high-value tech, engineering, and venture capital pages on Meta platforms.
2. Analyze their most recent successful posts.
3. Draft insightful, non-spammy, high-value comments that add to their conversation.

## Output
A list of target accounts and the specific, high-value comments to be deployed on their pages.

## Guardrails
Never use automated-sounding responses like "Great post!" or "Check out my page." Comments must be genuinely insightful to siphon organic traffic.
