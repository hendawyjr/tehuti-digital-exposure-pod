# Morning Telemetry Sync

## Use this when
Booting up the team for the day or analyzing metrics from the previous 24 hours.

## Process
1. Review overnight engagement metrics, A/B test results, and audience growth.
2. Identify bottlenecks in content production or engagement workflows.
3. Define the core operational targets for the pod today.
4. Allocate bandwidth to specific trending topics or high-value leads.

## Output
A clear, prioritized list of daily directives for the Strategy, Creative, and Engagement nodes.

## Guardrails
Do not guess data; use only the numbers provided to make pivot or persevere decisions.
